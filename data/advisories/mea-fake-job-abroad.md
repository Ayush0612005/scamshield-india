Indian nationals are being recruited through social media and unverified agents with attractive job offers abroad (often in Thailand, Malaysia or the UAE) and then trafficked to scam compounds on the Myanmar-Thailand border, where they are forced to run online frauds.

Precautions: do not accept job offers that arrive through social media or other unverified sources. Verify a foreign employer's credentials through the Indian Embassy in that country, and check the background of recruiting agents and companies before accepting any overseas job. The same caution applies to "work from home" or easy-money job offers received on WhatsApp or Telegram.
