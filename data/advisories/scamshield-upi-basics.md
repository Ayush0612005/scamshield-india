Your UPI PIN authorises money LEAVING your account. You never need to enter a UPI PIN, scan a QR code or approve a request to RECEIVE money. A message saying "enter your PIN to receive the refund, cashback or prize" is a scam.

A UPI "collect request" asks you to pay. Approving it with your PIN sends money to the requester. Scammers posing as buyers on OLX-style marketplaces, refund agents or customer care send collect requests disguised as payments to you.

OTPs, UPI PINs, card CVVs and passwords are never needed by bank staff, customer care, the police or RBI. Banks' own OTP messages explicitly say "do not share". Anyone asking you to read out an OTP is attempting fraud.

Fake KYC messages claim your account, wallet or SIM will be blocked unless you update KYC through a link or by calling a mobile number. Banks do not block accounts by SMS with a link. Use only the bank's official app or website, typed yourself.

Never install screen-sharing or remote-access apps (AnyDesk, TeamViewer, QuickSupport) or unknown .apk files at a caller's request. They let the caller see your OTPs and control your phone.

If money has been lost, call 1930 immediately or report at cybercrime.gov.in, and inform your bank to block the transaction.
