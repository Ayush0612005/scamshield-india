Chakshu, on the government's Sanchar Saathi portal, lets citizens report suspected fraud communication received by phone call, SMS or WhatsApp, for example fake KYC update messages, impersonation of officials, or fake offers. Report within 30 days so action can be taken against the numbers involved.

How to report: open Sanchar Saathi, go to Citizen Centric Services and choose Chakshu, select the medium (call, SMS or WhatsApp) and the category of fraud, describe what happened (a screenshot is optional), verify your own mobile number with an OTP sent by the portal, and submit.

Chakshu is for suspected fraud attempts. If you have already lost money, report to the cybercrime helpline 1930 or on cybercrime.gov.in instead. Ordinary spam calls and promotional SMS can be reported to 1909 or through the TRAI DND app.
