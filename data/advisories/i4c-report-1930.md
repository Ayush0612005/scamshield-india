If you have lost money to an online or financial fraud, report it immediately by calling the national cybercrime helpline 1930 or by filing a complaint on the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal at www.cybercrime.gov.in. Reporting quickly gives the best chance of the money being stopped.

Basic online-safety habits recommended by I4C: do not click pop-ups or links in emails and messages from unknown senders, use strong passwords and two-factor authentication, keep your phone and apps updated, and back up important files.
